How to play
  1. Goal: find all 9 blue Pokémon hidden on the 5×5 board.
  2. A handful of clues (up to 5) point to those blue tiles. The × number tells you how many tiles a clue covers – e.g. FIRE × 2 means two of your Pokémon to find are Fire-types.
  3. Clues can be about almost anything – Pokémon types, colours, the animals they're based on, places they're found, moves they use, trainers who've caught them. Sometimes you'll need to think outside the box.
  4. Tap a tile to guess it. Guess right and it turns blue. Guess wrong and it's a strike (turns yellow).
  5. You can make up to 4 mistakes – the 5th strike ends the game.
  6. Stuck? Tap 💡 Reveal an extra clue for a nudge toward a tile you still need.
  7. Take notes: once you think you've found all the Pokémon linked to a clue, tap that clue to grey it out. You can also tap / click & hold a tile to add colour notes – really handy on the harder days, where clues start to overlap.
  8. It ramps up: two new puzzles drop daily (one Gen I, one all-gens) and they get tougher through the week – Mon easy → Tue → Wed → Thu → Fri → Sat → Sun evil.
